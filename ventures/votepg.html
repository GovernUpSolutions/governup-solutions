Overview

VotePG helps Prince George’s County residents locate district-specific election and candidate information. Created in response to the difficulty of finding local election resources in one place, the platform was publicly available during the 2026 primary period and is intended to support relevant election cycles.

  • Address-based district and ballot discovery
  • Candidate and race information
  • Links and resources to support informed voting
VotePG is an independent civic-information initiative under GovernUp ownership. It does not endorse candidates, political parties, or ballot positions and is not an official election authority. Verify voting requirements with official election sources.
