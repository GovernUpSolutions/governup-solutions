Practical technology

Covenant is available to prospective communities and clients. Its tools support the everyday work of homeowners associations, from organizing documents and service requests to managing board and resident workflows. Features vary by plan; consult the product website for current availability.

  • Resident and board dashboards, announcements, and documents
  • Financial tools and service requests
  • Online voting, violation tracking, and architectural review workflows
Covenant is a software product of GovernUp Solutions, LLC. Product availability does not imply an established customer base or a management relationship with any association.
