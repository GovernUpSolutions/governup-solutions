Leadership

Blake Hemphill

Founder & Chief Executive Officer · GovernUp Solutions, LLC

Background

Blake Hemphill is an entrepreneur and organizational leader with experience in executive operations, community governance, technology development, and publishing. As Founder & Chief Executive Officer of GovernUp Solutions, LLC, he develops ventures that improve access to information, strengthen community institutions, and solve operational problems.

Blake is Founder & Publisher of The Westphalia Gazette and the founder behind Covenant, Covenant Management Partners, and VotePG. His work connects an interest in useful technology and accessible information with a practical understanding of organizational leadership.

Experience serving as an HOA board president helped inspire the association software and services ventures. The Gazette and VotePG respond to different needs: sustained local reporting and easier access to civic information.

His ambition is to build enduring businesses that strengthen community institutions, with clear boundaries between corporate ownership, independent journalism, and nonpartisan civic information.

Professional and community roles are biographical context. They do not imply government or homeowners association sponsorship, endorsement, or a client relationship.
